# Add authentic imagery to the main product functions

## Goal
Make eterfaceID’s core purposes immediately understandable through realistic, trustworthy photography while preserving the restrained financial-services design.

## Image set
Create three coordinated, original images in an authentic workflow style:

1. **Verify People** — a real customer completing identity verification on a phone with a government ID/passport visible naturally in the scene.
2. **Verify Businesses** — a founder or operations lead reviewing business registration information and ownership details on a laptop with credible office documents nearby.
3. **Screen Continuously** — a compliance analyst reviewing a sanctions or monitoring case at a workstation, conveying ongoing oversight without inventing readable interface details.

The scenes will use natural lighting, diverse and credible professionals, documentary framing, and subtle visual ties to the navy, cyan, off-white, and signal-red brand palette. No stock-photo clichés, staged handshakes, fake logos, or legible personal information.

## Placement
- Redesign the homepage’s three-function row so each function has a clear image, title, concise explanation, and existing destination link.
- Add the person-verification image to the Person Verification page near the opening explanation and checks.
- Add the business-verification image to the Business Verification page near the opening explanation and entity checks.
- Add the screening image to the AML Screening page while retaining the existing animated screening film as the product demonstration.
- Keep images cropped consistently and responsive across desktop and mobile, with descriptive alternative text and lazy loading below the opening view.

## Quality checks
- Verify every crop at desktop and mobile sizes so faces, documents, and devices remain visible.
- Confirm images do not make the homepage materially slower or compete with the two existing product films.
- Check contrast, spacing, image loading, reduced-motion behavior, and all affected page links.
- Confirm every edited page builds without errors and retains its existing metadata and single main heading.

## Technical details
- Generate the image set as project assets rather than hotlinking external photography.
- Reuse a focused responsive image treatment instead of duplicating layout code across pages.
- Keep all colors and borders tied to the existing semantic design tokens.
